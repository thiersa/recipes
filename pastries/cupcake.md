# Cupcake

- Pieces: 12
- Prep time: 15 min
- Bake time: 25 min
- Skill: easy

**Ingredients:**

- 150g unsalted butter, softened
- 150g caster sugar
- 150g self-raising flour
- pinch of salt
- 3 eggs
- 4 tablespoons milk
- 12-hole muffin tin, lined with paper cases

**Instructions:**

1. Set the oven to 190℃
2. Beat the butter in a bowl until softened.
3. Gradually add the sugar, flour, salt, eggs and milk to the mix and wisk
   until the whole mixture is smooth.
4. Use a tablespoon or an ice-cream scoop to divide the mixture between the
   paper cases.
5. Place the muffin tins in the oven and bake it for 20 min until the cupcakes
   are a light golden color.
6. Remove the tin from the oven and leave the cupcakes to cool inside the tin
   for a few minutes.
