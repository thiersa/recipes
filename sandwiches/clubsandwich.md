# Clubsandwich

![Clubsandwich](sandwiches/clubsandwich.jpg){ width=300px }

- Persons: 1
- Prep time: 10 min
- Skill: easy

**Ingredients:**

- 4 thin slices of (smoked) bacon
- 3 slices of chicken or turkey breast
- 3 slices of bread
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
- 1 hard-boiled egg, sliced
- 1 tomato, sliced
- a few salad leaves
- a handful of potato chips, if preferred

**Instructions:**

1. Spread the mayonnaise on two slices of bread
2. Spread the boiled egg slices on one of the bread slice
2. Put the bacon on top of the egg
3. Put the potato chips on the other slice
3. Put the chicken/turkey on the same slice and arrange the salad leaves on
   top of it
4. Put the 3rd slice of bread on top and finally put this on top of the 1st
   slice
