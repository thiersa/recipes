# French Omlet

- Persons: 1
- Prep time: 3 min
- Cook time: 5 min
- Skill: easy

**Ingredients:**

- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons of water
- olive oil or teaspoon of butter
- filling: shredded cheese, chopped ham

**Instructions:**

1. beat the eggs, water, oil and filling until it's blended
2. heat the butter in a frying pan
3. pour the eggs in the frying pan
4. fry the whole for 4-5 minutes
5. seasoning as preferred: pinch of salt
