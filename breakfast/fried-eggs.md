# Fried Eggs

![Fried Eggs](breakfast/fried-eggs.jpg){ width=300px }

- Persons: 1
- Prep time: 4 min
- Cook time: 3 min
- Skill: easy

**Ingredients:**

- 2 eggs
- olive oil or teaspoon of butter

**Instructions:**

1. heat the butter in a frying pan
2. crack the eggs in the pan, without scrambling
3. fry eggs for 3 minutes
4. seasoning as preferred: pinch of salt
